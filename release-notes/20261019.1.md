**Stop stays stopped.** With "Start Docker at Login" on, choosing Stop in the Container Tools menu
used to last at most a minute before Docker started itself again. Now it stays stopped until you
start it, or until you restart your Mac.

**See how full the Docker disk is.** The menu now shows the Docker VM's free disk space. When it is
90% full, the menu says so, the icon gets a badge, and you get a notification (at most once a day).
**Free Up Space…** removes unused images and build cache after showing how much that frees. It
never touches your containers or volumes.

**Download VMware Fusion in your browser.** **Get VMware Fusion…** now opens the download in your
web browser, so you can watch its progress, instead of downloading silently in the background.

This is also the first release installed and tested on a real Mac OS X 10.9.5 before publishing.
